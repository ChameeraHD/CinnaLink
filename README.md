**Connecting Sri Lanka's Cinnamon Workforce.** 

CinnaLink is a digital workforce connection and management platform built specifically for the Sri Lankan cinnamon industry by Team AlgoFive. By bringing landowners and skilled workers together digitally, fairly, and in real time, CinnaLink eliminates the need for middlemen and ensures a smoother, more transparent harvesting season.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧑‍🌾 For Landowners
*   **Easy Job Posting:** Post upcoming jobs with specific details, including job type, workers needed, pay rate, and duration.
*   **Wider Reach:** Publish ahead of the harvesting season to reach a far larger pool of workers than traditional word-of-mouth.
*   **Real-Time Tracking:** Track daily quill output and job progress straight from the field.

### 👷 For Workers
*   **Instant Matching:** Browse available jobs filtered by location, pay rate, and specific skills (cutting, peeling, rolling).
*   **Zero Double-Booking:** The system automatically checks your availability before allowing applications, eliminating scheduling clashes.

### 🤝 Trust & Community
*   **Fair Rating System:** A mutual, two-way rating system allows both landowners and workers to rate each other upon job completion, building long-term community trust.

---

## 🚀 Why It Matters

*   **No More Middlemen:** Direct communication ensures fair wages and transparent expectations.
*   **No Double-Booking:** Smart scheduling keeps everyone on track.
*   **Built to Scale:** Designed to handle the growing demands of the industry.
*   **Made for Sri Lanka:** Tailored specifically to the unique workflow of the Sri Lankan cinnamon trade.
